## 🎓 Helpful Class Links

### 📘 Dr. Behzad Razavi — Fundamentals of Analog Design
Dr. Behzad Razavi is one of the most respected professors in the field of analog and mixed-signal circuit design.  
His lectures are highly recommended for building a strong foundation in analog electronics.

**Topics Covered:**
- Diodes  
- MOSFETs  
- Single-Stage MOS Amplifiers  
- Current Mirrors  
- Differential Pairs and Differential Amplifiers  
- Frequency Response  
- Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps)  

🎥 **Lecture Series:** [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLyYrySVqmyVPzvVlPW-TTzHhNWg1J_0LU)

---

### ⚙️ Dr. R. S. Ashwin Kumar (IIT Kanpur, SSCD Lab) — Advanced Analog Design
For advanced analog circuit design concepts, refer to the lecture series by **Dr. R. S. Ashwin Kumar** from **IIT Kanpur (SSCD Lab)**.  
These lectures provide deep insight into advanced amplifier topologies and precision circuit design.

**Topics Covered:**
- Op-Amps (5T OTA, Telescopic, Folded Cascode, Gain-Boosted, etc.)  
- Two-Stage Op-Amps and Compensation Techniques  
- Frequency Response  
- Noise and Mismatch Analysis  
- Various Bandgap Reference Circuits and LDOs  

🎥 **Lecture Series:** [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLP-rjhz_nIi7UHSFzysn7LRLh1jSRj9SP)

---

### 🔄 Dr. R. S. Ashwin Kumar — Mixed-Signal Design
For mixed-signal circuit design, Dr. R. S. Ashwin Kumar also offers an excellent course that focuses on sampling and converter architectures.

**Topics Covered:**
- Switched-Capacitor Circuits  
- Sampling Circuits  
- Dynamic Comparators  
- Flash ADCs  
- SAR ADCs  
- Pipeline ADCs  

🎥 **Lecture Series:** [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLP-rjhz_nIi7TQ1Ks7d_whsRYw_bBfY9w)
